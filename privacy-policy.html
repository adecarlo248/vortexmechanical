Privacy Policy

Vortex Mechanical Inc. (“Vortex,” “we,” or “us”) provides HVAC services in Ontario. This policy explains how we handle personal information through vortexmechanical.ca, our booking tools, AI chat and related customer communications. Vortex is responsible for information under its control, including information processed by providers on its behalf.

Information we collect and why

Our appointment form requires your full name, email address, telephone number and full service address. We also collect the service issue and requested appointment window. We use these details to identify and contact you, locate the property, prepare for the visit, arrange appointments and respond to your request.

If you use our AI chat, we collect the messages you submit, contact details you provide and information about your service or booking request. Conversation history and contact details are stored through 1APP to support your inquiry and appointment arrangements. Related customer records include appointment details and communications about your request.

Provide only information needed for your service inquiry. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, Social Insurance Numbers, tax documents, income information or other sensitive financial information in our form or chat. Our heat-pump rebate page is informational: use official government application channels for rebate applications and supporting documents. Do not submit someone else’s personal information without their authority.

Bookings and communications

We use your information to answer questions, arrange service and email appointment confirmations to the booking contact. Booking details also generate internal email notifications to Vortex so we can handle the appointment. Information may therefore be included in booking records, chat history and customer-service emails.

Service communications are separate from optional marketing. Booking does not sign you up for marketing or constitute express marketing consent. If we introduce promotional emails or texts, we will obtain the consent required by Canada’s anti-spam legislation, identify the sender and provide an unsubscribe option. Declining optional marketing will not prevent you from requesting service.

AI chat

Our website chat is an automated AI assistant, not a person. It uses your conversation to generate answers, collect service details and assist with bookings through our third-party platform. AI responses can be inaccurate; contact Vortex directly to clarify an answer, correct information or discuss an appointment with a person.

Chat involves processing your messages by third-party technology. Limit what you share to necessary service details. You can call or email Vortex instead of using AI chat.

The homepage chat and booking form are loaded from leadconnectorhq.com, with a supporting script from msgsndr.com. This policy does not identify the model provider, how long that provider keeps messages, or whether messages are used for the provider’s own purposes.

Providers, website technology and sharing

We use 1APP for booking, contact records and chat, email services for communications, GitHub Pages for hosting and an embedded Google Maps feature. The pages also request fonts from Google Fonts. Providers receive information needed to deliver their features. Hosting and embedded services may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser or device details and request times when your browser connects to them.

Embedded booking, chat and map tools may use cookies or similar technologies to operate, remember a session or support security. Providers may also handle technical information under their own privacy notices. Google’s notice is at policies.google.com/privacy (opens in a new tab). You can manage cookies through your browser; blocking them may affect embedded features. Contact us for help requesting service without these tools.

We will limit sharing to identified service purposes, your authorization, or circumstances permitted or required by law, such as responding to a valid legal demand. Vortex remains responsible for information processed on its behalf and will require protection appropriate to that information.

Providers and supporting services may store or process information outside Canada. Information processed abroad may be accessible to courts, law-enforcement or national-security authorities under foreign laws. Contact us with questions about our providers and processing practices.

Consent and choices

We will explain collection purposes and seek consent appropriate to the information and circumstances, unless the law permits otherwise. Browsing our website is not blanket consent to unrelated uses of your information.

You may withdraw consent by contacting us, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. We will explain any consequences: without information needed to arrange service, we may be unable to complete a booking or respond to your request. Withdrawal does not automatically require deletion of records we must lawfully retain.

Protection and retention

We will apply reasonable physical, organizational and technical safeguards appropriate to the information’s sensitivity, including limiting access to those who need it for authorized purposes. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. We will address privacy incidents and make notifications required by applicable law.

We will retain personal information only as long as needed for identified purposes, including service records, resolving disputes and legal obligations. When no longer needed, we will securely delete, destroy or anonymize it, including addressing provider-held copies under our control.

Access, corrections and concerns

Contact Privacy inquiries, Vortex Mechanical Inc. at vortexmech.inc@gmail.com or 705-927-2262. Make access requests in writing; we can help you prepare one.

You may ask what information we hold about you, how it has been used or shared, request access or correction, or ask about deletion and consent withdrawal. We may reasonably verify your identity. We will respond within applicable statutory time limits and explain any permitted extension or restriction. Legal exceptions may limit access or deletion, including protecting another person’s information. We will explain available recourse and record unresolved correction concerns where required.

We will investigate privacy complaints. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (opens in a new tab).

Changes

We will post updates here, explain material changes and obtain additional consent where required before introducing a new use or disclosure.
