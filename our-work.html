Our Work

A look at Vortex Mechanical installation work, from outdoor comfort equipment to indoor heating systems.

These photographs show installation work by Jordan Underwood of Vortex Mechanical.

Outdoor equipment

Mitsubishi Electric outdoor installation

An outdoor Mitsubishi Electric unit mounted on a raised stand, with refrigerant lines routed along the wall. This equipment connects to an indoor unit as part of a split heating or cooling system.

Hydronic equipment

Navien hydronic equipment installation

Two wall-mounted Navien units with connected piping, circulation pumps and an expansion tank. Hydronic systems use circulating water to transfer heat.

Outdoor equipment

Lennox outdoor unit installation

A Lennox outdoor unit positioned beside the building, with electrical and refrigerant connections. Outdoor units exchange heat with the surrounding air as part of a connected indoor comfort system.

Indoor heating

Compact furnace installation

A ducted furnace installed in a dedicated closet, with visible vent piping and duct connections. A furnace supplies heated air through the connected ductwork.

Outdoor equipment

Raised Mitsubishi Electric outdoor unit

A Mitsubishi Electric outdoor unit installed on a raised metal stand, with connections routed up the exterior wall. The outdoor component works with connected indoor equipment as part of a split system.

Indoor heating

Lennox ducted furnace installation

A Lennox furnace connected to metal ductwork, with vent piping and a condensate drain visible. The furnace distributes heated air through the building’s duct system.
